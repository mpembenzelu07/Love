I Miss You

Some people are miles away,
but somehow they are always close to the heart.

I don't know how to explain it, but I miss you more than words can say. ❤️

Every little thing reminds me of you. Your smile, your voice, your presence...

I just wish you were here with me right now. Until then, just remember one thing:

You are always in my heart. ❤️

Forever thinking about you...
